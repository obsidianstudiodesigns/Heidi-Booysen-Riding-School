Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Heidi Booysen Riding School ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Heidi Booysen is our Information Officer under POPIA. Contact her on the details above about anything in this policy.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, such as:

  • your name and cellphone number
  • the rider's name, age and riding experience
  • the service you want to book, and the days and times that suit you
  • anything you add in a message, such as a nervous first-timer or a group size
  • for therapeutic riding: information you choose to share about the rider's health, abilities or support needs
  • for horse treat orders: the bags you want and your name

Health information

Information about a rider's health or disability is special personal information under POPIA. We only ask for it so we can plan sessions safely and suitably, we only use it for that purpose, and we do not share it with anyone else unless you ask us to or the law requires it. You can choose how much to share.

How the forms on this website work

The booking form and the horse treats order form do not store or send anything to us by themselves. When you press a send button, the form opens WhatsApp with a message already written. Nothing reaches us unless you send that message yourself. The treat order total is worked out on your own device.

Why we use it

  • to confirm bookings and answer your questions
  • to match each rider with a suitable horse and plan lessons, outrides and therapy sessions
  • to keep riders safe on the day, including knowing who to contact
  • to fill horse treat orders
  • to keep records we are required to keep by law

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Children

Many of our riders are children. Bookings for riders under 18 are made by a parent or guardian, who gives consent for us to use the child's details as described in this policy.

Photos

We sometimes take photos at the yard. We will ask a parent or guardian before sharing a recognisable photo of a child on Facebook or this website, and you can ask us to remove a photo at any time.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to in order to run your booking, in an emergency, or when the law requires it. The services we use to talk to you, such as WhatsApp and Facebook (Meta), process messages under their own privacy policies.

This website, cookies and third parties

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. To load, your browser fetches files from these providers, which may record your IP address and browser details in their standard server logs:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces
  • jsDelivr, which supplies the icons
  • Google Maps, which shows the map in the "Find us" section

Links to Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep booking messages and rider details for as long as you ride with us, and delete them when they are no longer needed. Records of payments are kept for as long as tax law requires, usually five years.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including keeping it on password-protected devices and accounts and only sharing it when needed.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you or your child
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to us using it
  • withdraw consent you have given

Contact us on the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
