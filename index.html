Hi, I'm Heidi.

I teach horse riding at Houtkloof in Paarl. Whether your child is four and has never touched a horse, or you are an adult who has always wanted to learn, we start where you are and build confidence one lesson at a time.

Lessons are fun, safe and caring, and the horses really are part of the family. You learn to groom them, lead them and look after them, not only how to ride.

Therapeutic horse riding

Sessions designed for children with physical, cognitive or emotional disabilities, with sessions for adults too.

The horse's steady, rhythmic movement works the whole body, while time with a gentle animal builds trust and confidence. Children who simply want to improve their skills are welcome as well.

  • Hand-eye coordination
  • Balance
  • Core strength
  • Motor skills
  • Spatial awareness

    WhatsApp 063 774 9148 or use the booking form. Share the rider's age and any riding experience.

    Fifteen minutes for R200, to see if riding is for you before you book lessons.

What to wear

  • Long pants, like jeans, leggings or jodhpurs
  • Closed shoes or boots with a small heel, no sandals or slip-ons
  • A riding helmet. Ask Heidi if you need to borrow one
  • Sunscreen, water and a warm layer for cold mornings
  • Long hair tied back, and no dangling jewellery

Life at the yard

Questions parents and riders ask

What age can my child start riding?

Children can start from age 4. Little riders begin on the lead rein with Heidi walking beside them. Teens and adults are welcome too.

Do I need any riding experience?

No. All beginners are welcome and no previous riding experience is needed. Riders who already walk, trot and canter can keep building their skills.

What is a trial session?

A 15-minute session for R200. It lets you see what we do, and whether your child enjoys horse riding, before you book a set of lessons.

How much are lessons?

Prices depend on whether you choose a private or group lesson. Send Heidi a WhatsApp on 063 774 9148 for the current rates.

Who are therapy sessions for?

Therapeutic riding is designed for children with physical, cognitive or emotional disabilities, and Heidi also offers sessions for adults. It helps with hand-eye coordination, balance, core strength, motor skills and spatial awareness. Tell Heidi a little about the rider when you book.

What should we wear?

Long pants, closed shoes or boots with a small heel, and a riding helmet. If you do not have a helmet yet, ask Heidi if you can borrow one.

Where are lessons held?

At Houtkloof in Paarl, Western Cape. Heidi sends directions when you book.
